# Interview sheet

One page for every interviewer. The aim is to learn how their work actually goes, not to test or sell an idea. Hypotheses are never shown or read out to the person you're talking to.

## Before the call
- Book 20 minutes. Say who you are and that there's nothing to sell.
- Ask at the start whether you may take notes (and record, if you want to). Write down the answer.
- Have this sheet and a blank note open. Nothing else: no slides, no mockups.

## Open broad (first 5 minutes)
Let them talk before you steer. Mark in your notes the moment you first steer (`<- I asked` is enough).
- "Tell me about your role. What does a normal week look like?"
- "What takes up more time than it should?"
- "What's the most annoying part of [the area they just mentioned]?"

## Ask about the past, not the future
Ask what they did, not what they would do. "Would you use…?" and "Would you pay for…?" get polite guesses.
- "When did that last happen? Walk me through it."
- "What did you do about it? What did that cost you, in time or money?"
- "What have you tried to fix it? Why did you stop?"
- "What do you use for this today? Do you pay for it?"
- "Who else is involved when this happens?"

## Don't
- Don't pitch, describe a solution, or ask them to react to one. If you catch yourself suggesting something, note `<- I suggested` so extraction can mark it as prompted.
- Don't lead ("Isn't it frustrating when…?"). Ask, then wait.
- Don't argue with or correct them. A contradiction is a finding.
- Don't fill silences. Count to five.

## Close with three asks
1. **Follow-up:** "Could I come back to you in a few weeks once I've talked to more people?"
2. **Artifact:** "Is there anything you could share that shows how you do this today: a spreadsheet, a form, a checklist?"
3. **Intro:** "Who else should I talk to about this? May I mention your name?"

Write down exactly what they agreed to. "Sounds great" is not a commitment.

## Notes
- Type their words as they said them. Rough is fine; don't tidy or summarise.
- Note the date, format (phone, video, in person, email) and length.
- Note what you promised (a thank-you, a summary) and what they promised you.
- Save the notes to `inbox/` the same day as `YYYY-MM-DD-<initials>-<firm>.md`, then run intake.
- Send the thank-you the same day.
